## What this site collects

This site is deliberately minimal.

**No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.** The site sets no
cookies of its own and embeds no third-party tracking scripts. Nothing about
your visit is profiled or shared.

**The contact form.** If you choose to write to us, we receive the name, email
address, company name and message you submit. That is all the form sends.

## How the contact form works

Submissions are delivered to our mailbox by **Web3Forms**, a form-delivery
service. Your message passes through their infrastructure in order to reach
us. Web3Forms acts as a processor on our behalf; see their privacy policy at
<https://web3forms.com/privacy>.

## Why we process it, and on what basis

We process what you send solely to read and answer your enquiry. The legal
basis is our legitimate interest in responding to someone who has contacted
us about our services, and — where it applies — steps taken at your request
prior to entering into a contract.

We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not sell or share them
with anyone beyond the processor named above.

## Server logs

GitHub Pages, which serves this site, processes technical data such as IP
addresses in order to deliver pages and protect the service. That processing
is carried out by GitHub under its own privacy statement, and we have no
access to those logs.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, its correction or
erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and you may object to
processing based on legitimate interest. Write to the address above and we
will respond within one month.

If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you have the right to
lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL
(<https://www.cnil.fr>).
